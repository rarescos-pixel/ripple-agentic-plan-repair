# Ripple Repair Card

The Repair Card is Ripple's low-density Alexa-first decision surface. It is intentionally money-first and implementation-jargon-free.

## Primary view

For the golden flight cascade the card renders conceptually as:

```text
5 commitments affected

$116 at risk     $42 repair     $74 net preserved

Top affected commitments
• Dinner reservation
• Pet care
• Airport pickup
+2 more

5 actions · 5 services · 3 people notified

[ Approve $42 repair ]
```

The underlying structured object is generated by `ripple.presentation.build_repair_card(plan)` and includes:

- `display_hint=inline`;
- three primary economic metrics only;
- at most three visible impacts plus a remaining count;
- action/service/people scope;
- irreversible/unresolved counts;
- an exact-approval decision label;
- voice summary and accessibility label.

The card deliberately does **not** put graph paths, hashes, idempotency keys, model names or infrastructure in the primary visual hierarchy. Those remain available in judge/debug evidence, not in the user decision surface.

## Voice parity

The same plan is summarized as:

> I found five affected commitments. $116 is directly at risk. I can repair the cascade for $42 and preserve $74 net. The repair notifies three people. I need your approval before I change anything.

This makes the voice interaction useful even with no screen while preserving a compact multimodal confirmation surface when a display is available.
