# Ripple Repair Card

The Repair Card is Ripple's low-density Alexa-first decision surface. It is intentionally money-first and implementation-jargon-free.

## Primary view

For the golden flight cascade the card renders conceptually as:

```text
5 commitments affected

$116 at risk → $42 repair → $74 net preserved

Top affected commitments
• Dinner reservation
• Pet care
• Airport pickup
+2 more

5 actions · 5 services · 3 people notified

[ Approve $42 repair ]
```

The underlying structured object is generated by `ripple.presentation.build_repair_card(plan)` and includes:

- `display_hint=inline`;
- a single compact `money_summary` plus the same three primary economic metrics;
- at most three visible impacts, using human-facing commitment labels rather than internal ids, plus a remaining count;
- action/service/people scope;
- irreversible/unresolved counts;
- one exact visual approval label (`Approve $42 repair` in the golden case);
- the equivalent voice approval prompt (`Approve the $42 repair?`);
- voice summary and accessibility label ending at the same approval boundary.

The card deliberately does **not** put graph paths, hashes, idempotency keys, model names or infrastructure in the primary visual hierarchy. Those remain available in judge/debug evidence, not in the user decision surface.

## Voice parity

The same golden plan is summarized as:

> One thing changed. I found five affected commitments. $116 is directly at risk. I can repair them for $42 and preserve $74 net. The repair notifies three people. Approve the $42 repair?

The sequence is deliberate: one change → downstream scope → money at risk → repair cost → net value preserved → exact approval. This makes the first spoken turn explain Ripple's product value before implementation details and keeps the display and voice surfaces on the same decision.

## Accessibility parity

The accessibility label carries the same scope, three money figures and exact approval question. A user who does not consume the primary visual hierarchy therefore receives the same economic decision, not a reduced or generic description.
