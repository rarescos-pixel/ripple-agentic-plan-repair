# Ripple — WOW Video Voiceover v2

**Visual lock:** 2:15 / 4050 frames / 30 fps  
**Purpose:** concise narration with deliberate silence; visible trace-bound UI carries detailed proof.

## Timecoded narration

**0:00–0:15**  
You gave an agent a promise. Then reality changed. Ripple doesn’t preserve a chat turn. It preserves what the human meant.

**0:15–0:33**  
That meaning becomes a versioned contract: a goal, invariants, assumptions that may change, and explicit authority. The first plan is bound to the world that produced it. No provider write happens before approval.

**0:33–0:48**  
Then Mom goes out. The assumption changes, but her access remains protected. Ripple doesn’t rewrite the promise. It repairs only the plan.

**0:48–0:59**  
Only the due delta executes. Ripple reads the provider back: one verified receipt, one write.

**0:59–1:02.8**  
Departure moves. Now two invariants are threatened.

**1:02.8–1:14.8**  
Understanding is not permission. Meaning confirmation and exact action approval are separate human authority gates.

**1:14.8–1:24.8**  
The old departure time arrives. Nothing fires. The stale schedule has no authority.

**1:24.8–1:36**  
At the new time, Ripple still waits. A timetable isn’t proof the family left. New evidence changes the world — and demands fresh approval.

**1:36–1:48**  
End the conversation. Start a new session. The same contract, state, plan and receipts return.

**1:48–1:53**  
Replay it. Two writes remain two.

**1:53–1:59**  
Independent completion evidence closes the promise: satisfied.

**1:59–2:08**  
The hero is a digital twin. The Alexa+ host is simulated. The AWS service evidence is live.

**2:08–2:15**  
The session is temporary. The promise isn’t. Tell Ripple what must remain true.

Final on-screen line, intentionally not narrated:  
**Ripple keeps the promise as the world changes.**

## Delivery direction

- English, calm and confident; product-film narration rather than advertising voice.
- Short clauses; precise consonants; no exaggerated trailer gravitas.
- Leave a perceptible beat after “Then reality changed.”
- At “Understanding is not permission,” reduce pace and make the contrast explicit.
- “Nothing fires.” is dry and understated, not dramatic.
- Pause between “End the conversation.” and “Start a new session.”
- “Two writes remain two.” should feel like a proof, not a slogan.
- Lower intensity for the evidence-class disclosure; increase warmth only for the final thesis.
- Natural-cadence timing check: threat line fits in ~3.38 s inside its 3.8 s window; evidence line fits in ~9.07 s inside its 11.2 s window at approximately 165 wpm.

## Audio acceptance

- Narration must never cover every frame; silence is intentional around threat, read-back, session death, and final line.
- VO remains clearly above the music/sound-design bed without aggressive compression.
- No narration may add a claim absent from the canonical trace or approved evidence classes.
