## Technical Implementation
Evidence:

## Design
- one utterance captures the changed fact;
- one compact proposal discloses every affected service, person, cost and irreversible action;
- one confirmation applies only to the exact displayed snapshot;
- material drift forces re-approval;
- unresolved work remains explicit.

## Potential Impact
Golden scenario: **5 commitments repaired, $116 direct loss avoidable, $42 recovery cost, $74 net direct cash preserved.** These are deterministic fixture values, not market claims.

## Quality of Idea
Ripple's primitive is cascading consequence repair, not generic Q&A or calendar rescheduling: one state change invalidates a dependency graph and only affected commitments are repaired under bounded authority.

## Evidence rule
Marketing copy does not count. Each important claim must map to executable tests, public runtime behavior, receipts/logs, or an explicit simulation disclosure.
