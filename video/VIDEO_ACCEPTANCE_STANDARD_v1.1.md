# Ripple Video Acceptance Standard v1.1

Status: CANONICAL QUALITY GATE CANDIDATE for the Ripple submission video.

This document supersedes v1.0 only by making it stricter. Every mandatory criterion in `VIDEO_ACCEPTANCE_STANDARD.md` v1.0 remains mandatory and is inherited by reference. Nothing in this file may be interpreted as weakening v1.0.

A cut may not be called GOOD / READY / FINAL / SUBMISSION-READY if any mandatory inherited or added criterion is FAIL or UNKNOWN.

Allowed states remain: `UNKNOWN`, `FAIL`, `PASS`, `N/A-EXPLAINED`.

## Locked hierarchy

1. Official organizer compliance.
2. Official judging criteria.
3. Winner / competitor benchmark.
4. Ripple proof-trailer gate.
5. Master technical gate.
6. Adversarial cold review.

Organizer requirements are the floor. Winner-caliber benchmarks are an additional stricter layer.

---

# V1.1 ADDITIVE HARD GATES

## H1. Dead-air / information velocity

- No interval longer than approximately 1.5 seconds may exist without a meaningful product-state, proof, interaction, visual, or audio-information change.
- A deliberate hold is allowed only when needed for readability or emotional comprehension.
- Decorative motion does not count as information change.

## H2. Real-product / evidence dominance

- Target at least 70% of useful runtime as real Ripple product footage or evidence directly derived from the frozen build.
- Title cards, generic motion graphics, architecture illustrations, stock imagery, and decorative transitions must remain subordinate.
- The most impressive visual moment must correspond to a real product state, verified output, or verified proof.

## H3. Continuous end-to-end proof

At least one contiguous segment must allow a cold viewer to follow:

`change -> cascade -> proposal -> exact approval -> execution -> receipts`

- Editorial latency compression is allowed.
- Intermediate product states may not be fabricated.
- Replay/idempotence may immediately follow this segment or be shown as a contiguous second proof beat.

## H4. Claim-to-evidence adjacency

- Every important technical or platform claim must be accompanied by evidence in the same beat or immediately adjacent beat.
- A claim may not rely on evidence shown tens of seconds later.
- Logos never substitute for runtime evidence.
- `VERIFIED`, `SIMULATED`, and `NOT CLAIMED` boundaries remain visually and verbally consistent.

## H5. Judge-condition readability

- All key values and claims must be readable at 720p and on an approximately 6-inch phone display without pausing.
- `$116 AT RISK`, `$42 REPAIR`, `$74 PRESERVED`, approval CTA, receipt result, and replay proof must each receive enough screen time to register.
- Captions may not obscure product proof.

## H6. Delayed memory test

Approximately 60 seconds after viewing, an unbriefed reviewer must still be able to recall without prompting:

1. one change causes a downstream cascade;
2. Ripple proposes a repair with concrete value;
3. the user gives exact approval before execution;
4. replay creates zero duplicate writes.

Failure on any of these four memory anchors is a hard FAIL until the cut is revised.

# V1.1 OPENING TARGET

The v1.0 proof-trailer gate remains valid and is tightened as follows.

Preferred target:

- 0:00–0:03 — real product + changed fact visible;
- by 0:05 — problem + product understood;
- by 0:10 — downstream cascade / differentiator understood;
- by 0:15 — verified economics visible;
- by approximately 0:20–0:23 — exact approval + execution proof established;
- by approximately 0:23–0:30 — receipts clearly visible;
- replay proof must appear early enough to remain one of the film's primary memory anchors.

The opening may use an aggressively compressed proof-trailer, but the film must then include a readable continuous real-product segment that demonstrates the causal sequence rather than merely repeating claims.

If speed reduces comprehension or readability, readability wins. The target is proof density, not frantic pacing.

---

# V1.1 RUNTIME TARGET

Hard rule: final master remains under 3:00.

Preferred competitive target: approximately 2:15–2:25.

A cut may extend toward approximately 2:35 only if:

- every additional second buys mandatory evidence or necessary readability;
- no mandatory proof can be communicated more clearly by editing rather than extending runtime;
- proof density, readability, and cold review remain PASS.

Never stretch toward 3:00 simply because the organizer permits it.

---

# V1.1 REPLAY MEMORY ANCHOR

Replay safety is a primary technical differentiator and must be visually simple enough for a non-technical judge to remember.

Preferred copy:

> Run it again. Nothing happens twice.

Verified proof surface:

- `5/5 DEDUPLICATED`
- `AUTHORITATIVE WRITES: 5 -> 5`
- `NEW PROVIDER WRITES: 0`

The film should communicate this property before deep architecture explanation.

---

# V1.1 SECOND-SCENARIO RULE

Event Operations remains optional and must only prove generality.

- Target <= 10–12 seconds.
- Show only the verified core economics: `$5,800 risk -> $620 repair -> $5,180 preserved` plus enough labels to prove the domain is not travel.
- No second interaction model.
- Cut the section entirely if it harms pace, comprehension, or memory of the golden mechanism.

---

# V1.1 OPTIMIZATION SCORE

Numeric scoring remains subordinate to hard gates.

After every mandatory gate is PASS:

- 48–50 / 50 = winner-caliber target;
- 46–47 / 50 = strong, revise before final;
- <=45 / 50 = not competitive enough for final master;
- 50 / 50 remains aspirational, not a substitute for evidence.

A 50/50 cut with one mandatory FAIL or UNKNOWN remains NOT READY.

---

# LOCK

Do not weaken this standard because a cut fails it.

It may be changed only if:

1. Amazon changes the rules or judging criteria;
2. a new credible winner/competitor benchmark materially improves the model;
3. a criterion is proven inappropriate or misleading for Ripple;
4. the owner explicitly requests a change.

The film changes to pass the standard; the standard does not change to pass the film.
