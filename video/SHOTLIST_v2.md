# Ripple submission video — shot list v2

Status: PREPRODUCTION. Capture timing is provisional until VIDEO FREEZE.

## Editorial principle

The product appears at frame 1. Spectacle is created by showing verified state transitions, not by delaying the demo with a generic trailer.

The viewer should be able to answer these questions without narration:
1. What changed?
2. What broke downstream?
3. What money is at risk?
4. What does Ripple propose?
5. What exactly does the user approve?
6. What actually executed?
7. What proof remains?
8. What happens on replay?
